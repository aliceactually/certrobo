# CertRobo PR #15 follow-up review — 2 November 2026

Reviewed head `c70f74c21cced006d15aaf3799d90571eba48449` against the previous reviewed implementation. The earlier serial-alias, issuer-isolation and wake-up cases now have targeted fixes and tests. Hold merge for the following two independently reproduced regressions.

1. **Reconciling equivalent serials silently deletes other certificate ledger records.** `RevokeCertificateAsync` (`Services/CertificateAuthorityService.cs:2262-2286`) calls `RemoveRange` on *every* other record with the same numeric serial, regardless of status, certificate PEM, subject, requester, or distinct history. `SeedRevokedSerialAsync` does the same at `:2340-2367`, selecting one primary and deleting the rest. In a disposable Linux test I inserted two issued ledger records with equivalent serials (`serial` and `00` + `serial`) but different subjects/requesters and non-empty certificate data; revoking the first succeeded and permanently deleted the second. The saved revocation occurs before publication, so loss also occurs if CRL publication fails. Preserve the existing ledger and audit evidence; group canonical serials for CRL purposes and report/reconcile genuine conflicts explicitly without silently discarding records. Add tests for both revoke and seed with multiple real issuance records, including publication failure.

2. **A passphrase-file error after saving revocation escapes instead of returning a pending outcome or waking the worker.** `TryPublishRevocationCrlCoreAsync` (`:2410` onward) creates a context, queries the issuer, and calls `EvictStaleRuntimeUnlockIfIncompatible` / `ResolveEffectivePassphrase` before its publication `try/catch` and before signaling pending work. With a configured passphrase file whose read permission was removed in a disposable Linux test, `RevokeCertificateAsync` threw `UnauthorizedAccessException` after persisting `Status=Revoked`; the previous CRL did not contain the serial and no wake signal was sent. Preserve accepted revocation intent, report a truthful pending/failure outcome for errors after the durable save, and wake/retry without hiding appropriate cancellation semantics. Exercise failures in the context/query/passphrase-resolution stages, not just the existing injector inside CRL generation.
